Getting started

  1. A parent creates a family account and selects a country and currency.
  2. The parent adds a child profile, chores, allowance, or both.
  3. To connect a child phone or tablet, open the child profile in the parent app, choose Link device, and share or scan the one-time invitation.

Account and data

Use Settings in the parent area to unlink devices, archive a profile, or delete the parent account and family data. Password recovery is available from the sign-in screen.

Contact support

Email sales@liisgo.com and include your device type, app version, and a description of the issue. Do not send a password or pairing code.
