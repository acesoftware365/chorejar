ChoreJar

Delete your account and family data

Last updated October 16, 2026

Only a parent who owns the ChoreJar family account can delete the family account. Deleting it removes the live parent account and family data that belongs to it. Service providers may retain limited information in secure backups according to their own retention practices.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to ChoreJar as the parent who created the family.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm.

After confirmation, ChoreJar removes the live family record, child profiles, chores, instructions, rewards, goals, allowance settings, money records, invitations and linked-device access. The owner’s sign-in account is also deleted. Service providers may retain limited information in secure backups according to their own retention practices.

If you cannot sign in

Email sales@liisgo.com from the email address on your parent account with the subject “ChoreJar account deletion.” Do not send your password. We will verify the request with the account owner before deleting the account and family data.

What may remain

We do not retain a separate public profile or a transaction history. Limited security, operational or legal records may remain only where required by law or necessary to protect our services. Advertising is not shown in the child area, and account deletion does not control data independently processed by an advertising provider under its own policy.
