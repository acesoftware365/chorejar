ChoreJar

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

ChoreJar is a parent-led family tool for organizing chores, allowance records and savings goals. It is a record keeper: parents manually record real-world events after they happen. ChoreJar does not transfer, hold, process, send, withdraw, cash out, lend, or pay money. It does not provide a wallet, payment account, bank account, financial product, cash reward, or child-initiated payout.

Information used to provide ChoreJar

When a parent creates a family account, ChoreJar uses the parent email address for sign-in. The family may add child nicknames, chores, instructions, rewards, goals, allowance settings and records of money that a parent has manually recorded. We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to authenticate the parent account and synchronize the family’s information between linked devices. Data is encrypted in transit.

A parent confirms that they are 18 or older before entering the parent account area. Children use a parent-controlled profile. A child does not need to provide an email address or password to use a linked child device. A child can see tasks, send tasks for review, choose a savings-goal picture and name, and follow their goal. A child cannot approve a reward, move money, request a payout, or record an expense.

Advertising

The free version may display one age-restricted, non-personalized, family-safe banner only in the parent area after adult confirmation. ChoreJar does not send names, chores, goals, balances or money records to advertising. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process an IP address (which can estimate general location), product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. The child area does not display ads. We configure the SDK for child age treatment and G-rated content, and do not use interest-based advertising or remarketing.

We do not sell or rent parent-account or family-profile data to advertisers. You can report an inappropriate ad from Plan and ads → Report an ad in the parent area, or contact us below.

How to manage or delete data

Parents can unlink child devices, archive child profiles, and delete their account and family data from Settings in the app. Deleting the account removes the live family information described in the confirmation screen and deletes the parent sign-in account. A parent can also request help with a privacy question at sales@liisgo.com. Service providers may retain limited information in secure backups according to their own retention practices.

Information we do not request

ChoreJar does not request bank account details, payment card information, contacts, precise location, or public child profiles. It does not offer messaging, social sharing, or public discovery between families.

Contact

For privacy requests or support, email sales@liisgo.com.
