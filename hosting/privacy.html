ChoreJar

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

ChoreJar is a family tool for organizing chores, allowance records and savings goals. It does not transfer, hold, or process money.

Information used to provide ChoreJar

When a parent creates a family account, ChoreJar uses the parent email address for sign-in. The family may add child nicknames, chores, instructions, rewards, goals, allowance settings and records of money that a parent has manually recorded. We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to authenticate the parent account and synchronize the family’s information between linked devices.

Children use a parent-controlled profile. A child does not need to provide an email address or password to use a linked child device.

Advertising

The free version may display child-directed, non-personalized ads only in the parent area. ChoreJar does not send names, chores, goals, balances or money records to advertising. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process device, diagnostic, and advertising data in order to deliver and measure ads. The child area does not display ads.

How to manage or delete data

Parents can unlink child devices, archive child profiles, and delete their account and family data from Settings in the app. Deleting the account permanently removes the family information described in the confirmation screen. A parent can also request help with a privacy question at sales@liisgo.com.

Information we do not request

ChoreJar does not request bank account details, payment card information, contacts, precise location, or public child profiles.

Contact

For privacy requests or support, email sales@liisgo.com.
