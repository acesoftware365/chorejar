# Promotional artwork — 1.0.1

This folder contains reusable promotional graphics for ChoreJar 1.0.1. The
artwork was copied forward from the approved project source without accounts,
ad creative, or personal family data. It may be used only after the account
holder confirms it owns or has permission to use every illustration and logo.

## Files

- `google_play/feature-graphic-*.png`: Google Play feature-graphic source.
- `google_play/app-icon-512.png`: required 512×512 Google Play app icon,
  rendered from the ChoreJar application icon.
- `app_store/promo-*.png`: App Store promotional-art source; App Store Connect
  does not use a feature graphic in the same way as Google Play.
- `social/portrait-*.png`: optional social promotion, not a store screenshot.
- `source/`: retained source renderings.

## Submission boundary

Do not submit these as device screenshots. Store screenshots must show the
actual 1.0.1 build on iPhone 17 Pro Max, use synthetic family data, show no ad
creative, and match the final English or Spanish text. Final captures are
listed under the versioned App Store and Google Play screenshot folders.
