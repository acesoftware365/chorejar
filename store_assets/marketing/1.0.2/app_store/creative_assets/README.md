# App Store Connect creative assets — 1.0.2

These are the final, ready-to-upload assets for the **Header and Search Results** section shown in App Store Connect. They were prepared from the official Apple dimensions and use flattened RGB PNG files with no alpha channel.

## Upload these files

| Placement | Exact Apple format | en-US | es-419 | es-ES |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product page Header | 21:9 · 3840 × 1646 px | `header-en-US-3840x1646.png` | `header-es-419-3840x1646.png` | `header-es-ES-3840x1646.png` |
| Search Results | 3:2 · 3840 × 2560 px | `search-results-en-US-3840x2560.png` | `search-results-es-419-3840x2560.png` | `search-results-es-ES-3840x2560.png` |

## Design and copy

- **en-US:** ChoreJar — *Small chores. Big dreams.*
- **es-419:** Mi Mesadita — *Pequeñas tareas. Grandes sueños.*
- **es-ES:** Mi Paga — *Pequeñas tareas. Grandes sueños.*

Each creative uses the actual jar and app icon from the project, an original abstract green-and-gold background, and text rendered locally in Nunito. There are no prices, subscription offers, ad creatives, URLs, account details, live balances, or personal family data.

## How to add them

1. In the version’s **Product Page Information**, open **Header and Search Results**.
2. Choose **Header** and upload the locale’s `header-…3840x1646.png`.
3. Choose **Search Results** and upload the locale’s `search-results-…3840x2560.png`.
4. Use **Preview** for iPhone and iPad, in each enabled language, before submitting the version.

The files under `alternatives/` preserve the initial art direction without replacing it:

- `initial-universal-16x9/` has the 5244 × 2950 PNG concept files.
- `initial-layout-concepts/` has early, non-upload layout studies.

Use the dedicated Header and Search Results files listed above for the App Store upload.

Source background: `source/abstract-green-gold-background.png`.
