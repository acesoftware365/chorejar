# ChoreJar 1.0.1 review packet

This folder is the submission worksheet for the signed `1.0.1 (11)` binary.
It records the store answers that follow from the implementation; it is not a
substitute for the account holder's legal agreements or any jurisdictional
legal advice.

Current evidence: the signed AAB and IPA have been inspected; the Google Play
store listing is saved as a draft in en-US and es-419. Use
`STORE_SUBMISSION_STATUS.md` for the exact remaining account-holder actions.

Before sending the build, compare the final AAB/IPA privacy reports against
these worksheets. If a report reveals an additional SDK data type, update the
appropriate store declaration and the public privacy policy before submission.

- `GOOGLE_PLAY_CONSOLE_ANSWERS.md` — Play Content, Families, Data safety and
  review access answers.
- `APP_STORE_CONNECT_ANSWERS.md` — App Store category, age-rating, App Privacy
  and App Review notes.
- `REVIEWER_NOTES.md` — the short explanation a reviewer needs to understand
  the parent-controlled family flow and the manual money records.
