# ChoreJar 1.0.1 — reviewer notes

ChoreJar is a parent-led household organizer. A parent or caregiver creates
the account, confirms they are 18 or older before the adult area opens, and
controls every family profile, invitation, money record, and linked device.

The child profile is limited to its own tasks and goal. It does not create an
account, provide an email or password, see advertising, approve rewards, move
money, request a payout, record an expense, or contact other users.

## Money terminology

Amounts are a private family record of real-world events. The parent manually
records a chore reward, allowance, money given, savings move, or expense only
after it has happened outside the app. ChoreJar never transfers, holds,
processes, sends, withdraws, lends, or pays money; it has no wallet, payment
account, bank account, cash reward, or child-initiated payout.

## Advertising

Only the adult parent area may show a single banner. It uses the Google Mobile
Ads SDK with child age treatment, G-rated content, non-personalized requests,
and no Android Advertising ID permission. The app disables the iOS same-app
key. No family-entered data is passed to advertising. The parent can open
**Plan and ads → Report an ad** to report an inappropriate creative.

## Review path

1. At the welcome screen, choose **I'm a parent** / **Soy mamá o papá**.
2. Confirm the adult parent area, then create an account with any review email
   and password.
3. Add a family profile and a task with a reward amount, then mark it reviewed.
4. From the parent money tab, record money given or move an existing balance to
   savings. These controls do not make any payment.
5. Use **Preview child view** or pair a child device with the QR/link/code.
   The child sees only tasks, money categories, and the savings goal.

No special hardware, payment method, phone number, child email, or reviewer
account is needed.
