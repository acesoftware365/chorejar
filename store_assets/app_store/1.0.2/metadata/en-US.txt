Name: ChoreJar
Subtitle: Chores, allowance & savings
Promotional text: Build family routines with chores, allowance records and savings goals.

Description:
Small steps, big dreams. ChoreJar helps your family organize chores and build habits of responsibility and saving.

Parents and caregivers create family profiles, assign chores with instructions and rewards, review completed tasks, set a weekly or monthly allowance, and record money they have already given outside the app. A parent-controlled child profile can see tasks, submit them for review, follow progress, and choose a savings goal.

ChoreJar clearly separates rewards in review, money owed, money available, savings, received money, and expenses. Every parent-recorded change stays in the history. A child cannot approve a reward, move money, request a payout, or record an expense.

ChoreJar is a household organizer and record keeper. It does not transfer, hold, process, send, withdraw, cash out, lend, or pay money. It does not provide a wallet, payment account, bank account, financial product, cash reward, or child-initiated payout. Real-world money or purchase decisions are made by a parent outside the app.

One app includes an adult parent area and a paired, parent-controlled child experience connected by a one-time code, secure link, or QR code. It works over the internet even when devices use different networks. Available in English and Spanish.

The adult parent area may show age-restricted, non-personalized, family-safe banner advertising. The linked child area contains no ads.

ChoreJar is a private household organizer and record keeper. A parent manually records a chore reward, allowance, money given, savings move, or expense only after the real-world event. The service never transfers, stores, or pays out funds. A child cannot request, withdraw, cash out, approve, send, or spend money in the app. Create any parent account for review, then add a child profile and use parent controls to review a task and record money given.
