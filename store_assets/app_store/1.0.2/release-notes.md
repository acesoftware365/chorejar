# ChoreJar 1.0.2 (12) — Release notes

- Refined the parent sign-in and account-creation flow for phones, tablets, and foldable displays.
- Made child device linking clearer with one-time QR codes, secure links, and pairing codes that work over Wi-Fi or different networks.
- Improved parent and child money and goal progress displays.
- Added clearer parent-only ad controls and privacy links; the linked child area remains ad-free.
- Improved English and Spanish regional allowance wording.

## Review disclosure

ChoreJar is a household organizer and record keeper. A parent manually records a chore reward, allowance, money given, savings move, or expense only after the real-world event. The service never transfers, stores, or pays out funds. A child cannot request, withdraw, cash out, approve, send, or spend money in the app.
