# App Store screenshots — 1.0.0

Capture screenshots from the signed release candidate on iPhone 17 Pro Max and
iPhone 16e, once the final account is populated with non-identifying demo
content. Provide English and Spanish sets, preserving the five narrative
screens listed in the Google Play screenshot guide.

`candidates/en-US/01-welcome-iphone-17-pro-max.png` is a clean capture of the
current welcome screen, with no account or advertising data. It is a review
candidate only: confirm the final App Store device-size requirement and final
release binary before uploading it.

`candidates/es-419/01-bienvenida-iphone-16e.png` is the equivalent clean
Spanish welcome capture from the normal application, not the local demo.
It likewise needs a final signed-release/device-size check before upload.

The staged Spanish parent/child narrative is mirrored in
`drafts/es-419/`. It contains five screens per role and is ready for visual
review before the final signed-release capture.

The matching English parent/child set is in `drafts/en-US/`.

Never submit the test ad banner, a test-ad creative, a real account email,
password, pairing code, or identifiable child data. Store screenshots must
reflect the final free parent/child experience and its actual features.
