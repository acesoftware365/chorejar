# ChoreJar 1.0.1 (11) — Review and release notes

Welcome to ChoreJar.

- Create parent-managed child profiles for chores, allowance records, and savings goals.
- Connect a child phone or tablet with a QR code, secure link, or pairing code.
- Let children follow their task and goal progress while parents keep control of every real-world money decision.
- Use the app comfortably on compact phones and foldable screens.
- Choose a country and see familiar local allowance terminology.

## Review disclosure

ChoreJar is a household organizer and record keeper. A parent manually records
a chore reward, allowance, money given, savings move, or expense only after the
real-world event. The service never transfers, stores, or pays out funds. A
child cannot request, withdraw, cash out, approve, send, or spend money in the
app.
