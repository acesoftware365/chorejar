# Google Play screenshots — 1.0.0

Use captures from **iPhone 17 Pro Max only** for the Google Play submission
sets. Duo and iPhone 16e remain valuable responsive QA devices, but their
captures are not store-upload sources. The required English and Spanish sets
should each show, in this order:

1. A parent assigns and reviews a chore.
2. A child submits a chore and sees their progress.
3. A child saves toward a goal or requests a withdrawal.
4. A parent reviews the money history.
5. A parent links a child by QR or code.

welcome-screen candidate. It is not one of the five narrative listing shots;
use it as a visual reference or add it only after confirming the final signed
release behaves identically.
