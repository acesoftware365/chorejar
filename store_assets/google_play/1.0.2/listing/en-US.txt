Full description:
Small steps, big dreams. ChoreJar helps families build responsibility and saving habits together.

Parents and caregivers create family profiles, assign chores with instructions and rewards, review completed work, set an allowance, and record money they have already given outside the app. A parent-controlled child profile can see chores, submit them for review, follow progress, and choose a savings goal.

ChoreJar keeps family records in clear categories: waiting for approval, owed, available, saved, received, and spent. Every parent-recorded change remains in the family history. A child can never approve a reward, move money, request a payout, or record an expense.

ChoreJar is a household organizer and record keeper. It does not transfer, hold, process, send, withdraw, cash out, lend, or pay money. It does not provide a wallet, payment account, bank account, financial product, cash reward, or child-initiated payout. Real-world money or purchase decisions are made by a parent outside the app.

Connect a child phone or tablet with a secure one-time QR code, link, or pairing code. Both devices can use Wi-Fi and do not need to be on the same network. A child profile does not need an email address or password.

The app is available in English and Spanish. Families choose a country during setup, and ChoreJar uses familiar regional allowance terminology.

The free parent area is for adults and may show age-restricted, non-personalized, family-safe banner ads after adult confirmation. The linked child area does not show ads.
