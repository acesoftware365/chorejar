# Local QA evidence — 1.0.0 (10)

These images are internal validation captures from the installed simulator
build. They prove responsive layout and the free parent area's test banner;
they are **not** store screenshots and must not be uploaded to Google Play or
App Store Connect.

- `iphone-16e-launch.png` and `iphone-17-pro-max-launch.png`: fresh launch
  checks on the two requested iPhone simulators.
- `iphone-duo-narrow-*.png`: compact Duo checks with the device closed.
- `iphone-duo-wide-parent-banner-landscape.png`: opened, landscape Duo check
  made with the separate Xcode 27.1 installation. The version label appears
  immediately above the Google test banner.

All advertising shown here uses Google's official test inventory. These
captures intentionally show `Test mode` and a local preview badge, so they are
excluded from the store-submission screenshot folders.
