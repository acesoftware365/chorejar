Name: ChoreJar
Subtitle: Chores, allowance & savings
Promotional text: Build family routines with chores, allowance records and savings goals.

Description:
Small steps, big dreams. ChoreJar helps your family organize chores and build habits of responsibility and saving.

Parents can create child profiles, assign chores with instructions and rewards, review completed tasks, set a weekly or monthly allowance, and record money given outside the app. Children see their tasks, submit them for review, and follow their money and savings goal.

ChoreJar clearly separates rewards in review, money owed, money available, savings, and expenses. Every change stays in the history. A child can request money from savings, and a parent must approve it.

One app includes a parent area and a paired child experience connected by a code or QR. It works over the internet even when the devices use different networks. Available in English and Spanish.

ChoreJar does not transfer money or process payments. Money is given outside the app and recorded by a parent. The free parent area may show non-personalized advertising; the child area contains no ads.
