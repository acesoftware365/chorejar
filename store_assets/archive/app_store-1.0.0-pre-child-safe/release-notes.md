# ChoreJar 1.0.0 — What’s New

Welcome to ChoreJar.

- Create parent-managed child profiles for chores, allowance records, and savings goals.
- Connect a child phone or tablet with a QR code, secure link, or pairing code.
- Let children follow their goal progress and request a cash out for parent approval.
- Use the app comfortably on compact phones and foldable screens.
- Choose a country and see familiar local allowance terminology.
