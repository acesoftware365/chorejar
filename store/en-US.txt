Name: ChoreJar
Subtitle: Chores, allowance & savings
Short description: Organize chores and allowance, record money given and support your child's goals.

Description:
Small steps, big dreams. ChoreJar helps your family build habits of responsibility and saving.

Parents can create child profiles, assign chores with instructions and rewards, review completed tasks, configure a weekly or monthly allowance, and record money given outside the app. Children see their tasks, submit them for review and follow their money and savings goal.

ChoreJar separates rewards in review, money owed, available money, savings and expenses. Every transaction stays in the history, and corrections preserve the original record.

One app with parent access and a paired child experience. Available in English and Spanish.

ChoreJar does not transfer money or process payments. Allowance periods are recorded when the parent area refreshes; money given always requires the parent's confirmation.
